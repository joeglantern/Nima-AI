# Help

**What changed:** 7 November 2026. First version.

Nima is a fashion app for Kenya. Independent shops hang their pieces on a rail, you see how a piece looks on you, and you pay by M-Pesa. If something here does not answer your question, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and a person will reply.

## Signing in

You can look around without an account. To save, buy, post or open a shop, you need one.

- **Email.** Enter your address and we send a six digit code. Type it in and you are signed in. No password is needed, though you can add one later from Settings.
- **Apple.** On an iPhone, tap Continue with Apple. You can hide your email address and it still works.
- **Google.** Tap Continue with Google and pick the account.

If a code does not arrive, check the spam folder and that the address is spelt right. You can ask for a new code after a minute.

## Orders and M-Pesa

When you check out, you enter the Safaricom number to pay from. A prompt appears on that phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN and the order is placed. Nima never sees your PIN.

- **No prompt came.** Wait a minute and tap Try again. Prompts can be delayed when the network is busy.
- **The prompt expired or you cancelled it.** Nothing was taken. Start the payment again from the order.
- **Money left but there is no order.** This can happen if the prompt was paid after it timed out. Send the M-Pesa message you received to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we either place the order or send the money back.
- **Collecting.** Most orders are collected from a Pick Up Mtaani point you choose at checkout. You get a message when it is ready. Bring the collection code from the order screen.
- **Something is wrong with the piece.** Contact the seller from the order screen first. If that does not sort it out, write to us with the order number.

## Seeing a piece on you

Try on uses a photo of you to show how a piece would look. It is a picture to help you decide, not a fitting, so sizes and fit can differ from the result.

- The photo and every try on are private to your account. Nobody else can see them unless you publish one as a look.
- To make the picture, the photo is sent to our image provider, Google, together with a description of the outfit. It is not sent with your name.
- To delete a photo, open Photos from your profile and remove it. Try ons made from it stay until you delete them too.

Try ons use credits. If a try on fails, the credits go back to you.

## Looks, shops and people

- **Save a piece** from any look or shop and find it under Saved.
- **Follow** a person or a shop to see their new looks first.
- **Open a shop** from your profile. List from your phone and Nima turns an ordinary photo into a catalogue shot.

## Reporting and blocking

If a look, a comment or a person breaks the rules, report it. On a look, scroll down and tap Report. On a profile, open the menu at the top and tap Report. Choose what is wrong and we look at it. The person is not told who reported them.

To stop someone seeing your profile, your looks or messaging you, open their profile and tap Block. They are not told.

For anything urgent, such as a photo of you posted without your permission, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the link.

## Deleting your account

Open Settings and tap Delete my account. You are signed out everywhere at once, and the account is erased after 14 days. Sign in again within those 14 days to keep it.

What is erased: your profile, photos, try ons, wardrobe, looks, likes, saves, comments, follows, messages you sent, conversations with Nima, sessions and sign in methods, along with the stored files behind them.

What stays: orders, payments and the records a shop needs to account for what it sold. They are no longer tied to your name or email.

## Contact

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Include the email address on your account and, for an order, the order number. We reply within two working days.
