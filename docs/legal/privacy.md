# Nima privacy policy

**Draft for a lawyer to review. Not in force and not legal advice.**

**What changed:** 7 November 2026. Named the providers, described deletion as the code does it, listed the cookies, and added the help page as the contact route.

## The short version

- Your photos and try-ons are private unless you publish them.
- To make a try-on, your photo is sent to Google, our image provider.
- Sellers get the details they need to fulfil your order.
- We process your data under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, and you can ask to see, correct or delete it.

## Who is responsible for your data

Nima AI, Nairobi, Kenya, is the data controller. The company's registered name, address and registration with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner will be stated here once the company is registered.

You can reach our data protection contact at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}, which is also the address for anything else. Put "Data protection" in the subject line for a data request.

Nima is for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that we hold data about someone younger, we will delete it.

## What we collect

**When you browse as a guest**
- A guest identifier, plus what you like, save, view and add to your cart, so these carry over if you create an account.

**When you create an account**
- Your name and email address.
- If you sign in with Google or Apple, the details they share with us.
- Your sign in sessions, including IP address and device or browser type.

**Your profile and style details, if you add them**
- Your handle, display name, bio, link and profile picture.
- Birth year, gender, county and city.
- Style preferences, budget, sizes, height and measurements.

**Your photos and images**
- Photos of yourself that you upload for try-on, and the try-on images made from them.
- Photos of your own clothes, if you add them to your wardrobe.

**What you do and say on Nima**
- Looks, lookbooks, likes, saves, comments, reviews, follows, blocks and reports.
- Messages you send to other people.
- Your conversations with Ask Nima, our AI stylist.

**When you buy**
- What you ordered, and your order history.
- Recipient name, phone number, delivery method, pickup point and delivery notes. If you choose doorstep delivery, the delivery details you give us.
- Payment records, such as the amount, status, M-Pesa receipt number and the phone number the prompt was sent to. Safaricom handles the payment itself; no PIN ever reaches us.
- Your credit purchases and balance.

**If you sell**
- Your shop details, listings and payout phone number.
- Your sales, fees and payouts.

**How you use Nima**
- First party events, such as views and clicks on posts and pieces, the screen they came from, and the platform you use. We collect these ourselves, not through outside analytics companies.
- A push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications.

## AI and your photos

When you ask for a try-on, we send your photo and a description of the outfit to Google, through its Gemini API, which generates the image. The photo goes with the outfit description only, not with your name or account details. We use the same service to process some other images, such as wardrobe items and product photos.

Google's terms for paid use of that API say it does not use what is sent to improve its models. The try-on it returns is stored in your account with us. Your photos and try-ons stay private, and links to them are signed and expire. They become visible to others only if you publish them.

Your feed is ordered partly by automated recommendations, using what you do on Nima. These do not produce legal or similarly significant decisions about you.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data.

- **Sellers.** When you order, the seller sees your display name, what you ordered, the delivery method and the order's progress, so they can prepare it. They do not see your phone number or email address.
- **Pick Up Mtaani.** When an order is collected from a pickup point, Pick Up Mtaani gets the recipient name and phone number you entered, so it can hand over the parcel and send you a message when it is ready.
- **Other people on Nima.** They see your public profile and anything you publish. People in a conversation see your messages. If your profile is private, only followers you accept see looks you mark for followers.
- **Event vendors.** Reports for event vendors use only data customers provided.
- **Safaricom (M-Pesa).** It processes the payment to Nima's Till and sends us the result.
- **Google.** It makes try-on images, as described above, and it provides sign in if you choose Google.
- **Apple.** It provides sign in if you choose Apple, and the App Store if you installed Nima from it.
- **Our email service.** It sends sign in codes and other email on our behalf.
- **Our hosting provider.** It runs the servers Nima lives on.
- **Authorities and advisers.** We share data when the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or to deal with a legal claim.

## Sending data outside Kenya

Google processes try-on images outside Kenya. Our email service and hosting provider may also process data outside Kenya. When that happens, we rely on the safeguards the Data Protection Act 2019 requires, or on your consent where the law asks for it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is open and for as long as we need it for the reasons above.

- **Guest data.** A guest record that never becomes an account is kept until we clear it. We intend to clear guest records that have not been used for 30 days, and this policy will say so once that runs.
- **Payment, order and tax records.** Kept for as long as the law requires us to keep accounting records.
- **Sessions.** A session lasts up to a month without use, then expires. Deleting your account ends every session at once.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Settings in Nima. We sign you out everywhere straight away, and you have 14 days to change your mind by signing back in. After that, we erase the account.

**What we delete:** your profile, style answers and measurements, photos, try-ons, wardrobe, looks, lookbooks, likes, saves, comments, follows, blocks, notifications, push tokens, your conversations with Ask Nima, the messages you sent, your sessions and sign in methods, along with the stored files behind them.

**What we keep:** orders, payments, the credit ledger and the sales records a shop needs to account for what it sold. These are no longer tied to your name or email: the account is marked as deleted and its name and email are replaced. An order can still contain the recipient details given at checkout. Reviews you wrote stay, shown as from a deleted account. If you opened a shop, its listings stay until you close the shop or ask us to. Usage events stay as counts, without your account linked to them.

## Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2019, you can:

- be told how your data is used
- get a copy of your data
- have wrong or misleading data corrected
- have your data deleted
- object to how we use your data
- withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect what we did before
- ask us to give you your data in a format you can move elsewhere, where the law allows

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to use any of these. We may need to confirm that it is you first.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

## Keeping it safe

Your photos are kept in private storage, and links to private files expire. We limit access to our servers and keep secrets off shared systems. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep access to your email account secure, since a sign in code goes there. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the Commissioner as the law requires.

## Cookies and similar technology

On the web, Nima sets the cookies it needs to keep you signed in: a session cookie, a short lived copy of your session details that saves a database lookup, and, during a Google or Apple sign in, a cookie that ties the sign in back to your browser. Their names start with "nima". The app on your phone keeps the same session in its secure storage instead of a cookie. Our first party events use a guest identifier. We do not use outside analytics companies or advertising cookies.
